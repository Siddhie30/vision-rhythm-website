Meet your teacher

Siddhi Farkandkar

Siddhi Farkandkar is the founder and lead instructor at Vision Rhythm, teaching Semi-Classical, Hip-Hop and Bollywood dance to students of all ages in Moshi. Her classes are built around clean technique, musicality and confidence on stage — taught in a way that's encouraging for absolute beginners and challenging for advancing dancers.

Every batch is kept small so each student gets individual correction, and every style is taught with respect for where it comes from — the grace of classical form, the sharpness of hip-hop, and the storytelling of Bollywood.
